Simulating radiation damage in fusion reactor walls

Volunteers lend spare computer time to simulate what happens when a fast neutron knocks a tungsten atom out of place: a collision cascade lasting a few trillionths of a second. Each work unit counts the defects the cascade leaves behind. Thousands of them tell us how wall materials wear out.
